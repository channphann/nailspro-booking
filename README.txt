NAILS PRO PUBLIC WEBSITE — V13

This package prepares the Nails Pro booking site for public hosting and Twilio A2P 10DLC review.

Included:
• index.html — booking website
• privacy.html — public Privacy Policy
• terms.html — public Terms & Conditions
• google-reviews-qr.png — Google Reviews QR image

The home page now links to Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions in the SMS opt-in area and footer.

Twilio requirements:
• The website must be publicly reachable without login.
• The website must clearly identify Nails Pro.
• The opt-in flow must be visible.
• Privacy Policy and Terms must be publicly accessible.
• Privacy Policy includes the required mobile-number non-sharing statement.
• Terms include message frequency, message/data rates, STOP/HELP, support contact, and carrier disclaimer.

IMPORTANT:
These policies are a practical SMS-compliance template, not legal advice. Review them for your business and have legal counsel review if appropriate.

NEXT:
Host this folder on a public HTTPS domain. Then use the public home-page URL in Twilio's Business website URL field. Do NOT use the Google Apps Script /exec URL as the business website URL.
