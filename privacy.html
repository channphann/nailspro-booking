Nails Pro 11 LLC — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Nails Pro 11 LLC ("Nails Pro," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use information when you use our appointment booking website and related services.

Information We Collect

When you request an appointment, we may collect your name, mobile phone number, appointment date and time, selected service, technician preference, and optional special requests. We may also keep appointment status information so we can manage your booking.

How We Use Information

We use appointment information to schedule and manage services, contact you about your appointment, send appointment confirmations and reminders when you have opted in to receive them, respond to support requests, and maintain our business records.

SMS Privacy

If you voluntarily check the SMS consent box on our booking form, Nails Pro may send appointment-related text messages such as booking confirmations, reminders, changes, and service follow-up messages. Message frequency varies based on your appointments. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in information, or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate our booking, spreadsheet, messaging, website, and related business systems. These providers may process information only as needed to provide services to Nails Pro and are not authorized to use your SMS consent for their own marketing.

Data Retention

We retain appointment information as reasonably necessary to operate the business, maintain records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Your SMS Choices

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to an SMS message. For help, reply HELP or contact Nails Pro Support at (859) 779-5925.
