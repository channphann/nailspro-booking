Nails Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Nails Pro respects your privacy. This policy explains how information submitted through our appointment-booking website is used, including information used for appointment-related text messages.

Information We Collect

When you request an appointment, we may collect your name, mobile phone number, requested service, preferred technician, appointment date and time, and any special request you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

We use this information to process appointment requests, communicate about appointments, provide customer service, and send appointment-related notifications when you have given consent to receive them.

SMS and Mobile Information

If you separately opt in to receive SMS messages from Nails Pro, we may use your mobile phone number to send appointment confirmations, reminders, scheduling updates, cancellations, and customer-care messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or SMS consent data with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers to help deliver messages and operate our booking system, but they may use the information only to provide services to Nails Pro and as permitted by applicable law.

Your Choices

SMS consent is optional. You can still request an appointment without agreeing to receive appointment-related text messages. If you no longer want SMS messages, reply STOP to an applicable message. For help, reply HELP.

Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information submitted through our website. No method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for appointment management, customer service, business records, legal obligations, and dispute resolution.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your SMS consent, please contact Nails Pro through our appointment website.
